The quiet craft that completes the home.

Handcrafted millwork for the residences of Atelier Pacifica — and for those who seek the same intentional beauty.

Stewards of the detail.

Within the homes that whisper elegance, the cabinetry must speak the same language. At Pacifica Workshop we craft kitchens, baths, and built-ins that honor the architecture — natural organic stone, rift white oak, walnut, and the play of California light.

Every joint, every finish, every hardware choice is considered. We work closely with the architects and designers of Atelier Pacifica, and with private clients who value the same quiet precision.

Not speculative. Deeply intentional.

Materials

Rift White Oak

Straight, quiet grain. Soft matte finishes that catch light without glare. The preferred pale wood for our California residences.

Walnut

Rich, warm depth. Used for islands, accents, and full kitchens where the architecture calls for grounded contrast.

Natural Organic Stone

Limestone, marble, and other stones with natural movement. Pairings that feel inevitable with the wood and the light.

The Process

01

Conversation

We begin with the architecture and the life of the house. Site visits, material palettes, and the specific rhythms of light and use.

02

Design

Detailed drawings, material samples, and hardware selections refined until every plane feels inevitable.

03

Craft

In our workshop, joinery, finishing, and assembly are executed with the same care given to the homes themselves.

04

Installation

Precise placement so the cabinetry reads as part of the architecture, not added to it.
